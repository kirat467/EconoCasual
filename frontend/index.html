        Try a customer

        Set a customer profile and see how likely a purchase is. The estimate comes from a simple logistic regression fitted in your browser on the sample records.

        Estimated chance of purchase –

        The model only learns patterns in this sample. A higher estimate with a discount does not prove the discount caused the purchase.

        Who should get a discount?

        Choose what share of the selected group can be offered a discount. The model ranks customers by how much a discount is expected to raise their chance of buying, and compares that with handing discounts out without a ranking.

        The model assumes a discount gives everyone the same push on the odds of buying, so the estimated lift is largest for customers who are neither very likely nor very unlikely to buy anyway. These are estimates from observed data, not a tested result. Try a small pilot before a full rollout.
