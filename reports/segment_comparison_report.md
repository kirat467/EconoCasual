# Customer Segment Comparison Report

## 1. Overview

- Total customers represented in comparison: 1000
- Total purchases: 573
- Overall purchase rate: 57.30%

## 3. Key Findings

- Highest purchase-rate combination: High Engagement + High Purchase History (80.17%).
- Lowest purchase-rate combination: Low Engagement + Low Purchase History (34.19%).

## 4. Interpretation

These results describe associations between customer segments and purchasing behavior. They do not establish that engagement or previous purchases cause a customer to buy.

## 5. Limitations

- Segment purchase rates depend on the available dataset.
- Small customer groups can produce unstable rates.
- Findings should be validated before business decisions are made.
